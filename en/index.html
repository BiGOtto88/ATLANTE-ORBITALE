See where the satellites are, right now

Keplario shows the Earth, the Solar System and over 15,000 satellites in orbit, at true scale and in real time. Nothing is enlarged: the ISS is a dot because it really is one. Move in and, for about thirty spacecraft, the real 3D model appears.

  • Pick a satellite or a probe and read its orbit, position, speed, shape and mission.
  • Follow 31 probes, from the Moon to Voyager 1, with the ground track on the planet they orbit.
  • Find out when the ISS passes over you and whether you can see it with the naked eye.
  • Design a mission: orbit, attitude, perturbations, power, links and launch windows to the planets.
  • Ask the assistant a question.

Free, no sign-up, on computer and phone. Orbital data come from CelesTrak and update every day; the calculations use the models of space engineering (SGP4, IGRF, NRLMSISE-00).

Keyboard: arrows to rotate · + and − to zoom · space to pause.

Where does the data come from?

Satellite orbital elements are from CelesTrak, probe positions from NASA/JPL Horizons, stars from ESA's Hipparcos catalogue. The 3D models come from the public NASA 3D Resources collection. All sources and licences are listed in LICENZE.txt.

Can I use it as an app?

Yes. You can install it from the browser on your phone or computer and, after the first visit, it also works offline with the latest data it downloaded.
